## Notes on sourcing

- AT89S52 is chosen over AT89C51 because it is programmed through SPI with a USBasp, which costs about INR 250, instead of a parallel programmer that costs several thousand rupees.
- 74HC573 is a transparent latch; the 74HC373 or 74LS573 also work but HC is preferred for CMOS 5 V logic and low loading on the P0 bus.
- Substitute MAX232A and use 0.1 uF charge pump capacitors, or use MAX3232 with 0.1 uF capacitors for 3.3 V tolerant operation.
- Any 16x2 HD44780 compatible display works; JHD162A, WH1602 and generic blue or green backlight modules are interchangeable.
- Any common cathode 2 digit 0.56 inch display works. A common anode display does not work with this driver and must not be substituted without changing the segment table and digit driver polarity.
